Our story

Built One Answered Call at a Time

Alex Morgan opened Brightwater with one truck and a phone that never went to voicemail. Too many neighbors had stories about waiting days for a plumber who never called back. The fix seemed obvious: pick up, give a straight answer and get someone there.

The company has grown since then, with more trucks, more technicians and customers across the state, but the rules have not changed. Every technician explains the problem before quoting it. Every quote is approved before work starts. Every job ends with a test and a clean floor.

We still think of ourselves as a neighborhood shop. Most of our crew lives within a short drive of Albany, and plenty of our customers have called us for a second, third and tenth time.

Alex Morgan, Owner and Licensed Master Plumber.
