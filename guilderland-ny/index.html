Aging builder grade water heaters

Kitchen line clogs in split levels

Suburban and Rural Plumbing in Guilderland

McKownville and Westmere grew up around Western Avenue and Route 20 from the 1940s on, with Capes and ranches near the city line and larger colonials and split levels farther west. The sandy Pine Bush soil drains well, but it lets tree roots travel a long way to reach a sewer joint. Camera inspections help here.

Past Guilderland Center, homes thin out toward Altamont and the escarpment. Older village houses, farmhouses and newer homes on acreage rely on private wells and septic in many spots. Those systems need plumbing choices that protect the well and keep solids out of the tank.
