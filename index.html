Plumbing and Drain Cleaning for New York Homes and Businesses

Quick answer: Brightwater Plumbing and Drain is a 24/7 plumbing company based in Albany, NY. We clear drains and sewer lines, repair and replace water heaters, fix frozen and burst pipes, find leaks and repair fixtures for homes and businesses across New York, with upfront pricing and insured technicians.

Pipes break on their own schedule, so we keep ours open. Brightwater was built on taking the calls that other shops let go to voicemail: the toilet that overflows during a dinner party, the water heater that quits on the coldest morning of the year, the sewer line that backs up into a finished basement on a holiday weekend.

Every visit follows the same pattern. Your technician finds the cause, explains it in plain language, lays out the options and gives you a price before touching a wrench. When a pipe is out of sight, our camera puts it on a screen so you can see the problem yourself. Then we fix it, test it and clean up.

Plumbing in this part of the country takes a beating. Century old cast iron, clay sewer laterals, hard water and long freezing winters each cause their own failures. Our technicians work on all of it every week and stock their trucks to match.
