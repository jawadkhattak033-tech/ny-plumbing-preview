Local conditions

Plumbing Problems We See Most in the Southern Tier

Flooding along the rivers

Homes near the Susquehanna and Chemung take on water in big storms and spring melt. Sump pumps, backwater valves and relocating water heaters off the floor all reduce the damage next time.

Well pressure and hard water

Hill town homes depend on wells that often carry hard water or iron. Waterlogged pressure tanks, short cycling pumps and scaled water heaters are common problems.

Old drains in river city homes

Early 1900s homes in Binghamton, Elmira and Johnson City often still drain through cast iron and clay. Cracks and roots cause backups that a camera inspection can locate.

Hard use in student rentals

Rentals around Ithaca and Binghamton see heavy use, clogged drains and worn fixtures. Landlords benefit from planned maintenance timed around move in and move out.
