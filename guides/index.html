What Plumbing Repairs Cost and Why

We do not publish one-size prices because two jobs with the same name can be very different. These guides explain what moves the price up or down.

Cost guide

What Drives the Cost of Drain Cleaning

The biggest factor is where the clog sits and how we reach it.

  • A single fixture trap is a short visit; a main line takes heavier equipment.
  • An accessible cleanout keeps the price down. No cleanout means pulling a toilet or going through a roof vent.
  • Repeat clogs may need a camera inspection to find the real cause.

Cost guide

Water Heater Replacement: What Changes the Price

Tank size and fuel type matter, but so does everything around the heater.

  • Gas, electric and tankless units carry very different equipment costs.
  • Venting, gas line and code upgrades can add to the job.
  • Tight basements and stairs affect how long removal and install take.
Water Heater Replacement service
Cost guide

Sewer Line Repair: Spot Fix or Full Replacement

A camera inspection tells you which one you are dealing with.

  • A single cracked joint can often be repaired without replacing the whole line.
  • Depth, length and what sits on top (driveways, trees, patios) drive excavation cost.
  • Trenchless methods can cost less overall when digging would mean restoring landscaping.

Winter guides

Cold Weather Plumbing Guides

Long, hard winters are tough on pipes. A little prep in the fall saves a lot of water damage in January.

Emergency guide

Burst Pipe? Your First Ten Minutes

Fast action keeps a repair from becoming a renovation.

  • Shut off the main water valve. Know where it is before you need it.
  • Turn off power to any wet area at the breaker if it is safe to reach.
  • Open faucets to drain the lines, move valuables, then call us and take photos for insurance.
Burst Pipe Repair service
Prevention guide

How to Keep Pipes From Freezing

Most frozen pipes are on outside walls, over garages or in crawl spaces.

  • Disconnect hoses and shut off outdoor spigots from inside before the first freeze.
  • Open cabinet doors on outside walls during cold snaps and keep the heat at least in the mid 50s.
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated spaces and seal drafts near them.
Frozen Pipe Repair service
Business guide

Winterizing a Commercial Building

Vacant units and seasonal closures are where freezes cause the most damage.

  • Keep heat on in vacant units and check them on a schedule.
  • Drain and shut off supply to unheated areas, irrigation and exterior fixtures.
  • Test sump pumps and backflow devices before snowmelt season.
