What we see most

Common Plumbing Problems in Mechanicville Homes and Businesses

Century old sewer connections

Many laterals here were laid around the time the houses were built. Cracked clay, missing sections and old connections at the street main cause slow drains and sewage odors in cellars.

Sewer Line Repair

Two family homes on one water line

Upstairs and downstairs units often share an undersized main service. When both run water at once, pressure drops, and old galvanized branches make it worse.

Repiping

Pipes splitting in cold cellars

Stone and brick foundations in older homes let cold air in along the sill. Supply pipes near the foundation wall can freeze and burst during a long cold snap.

Burst Pipe Repair

Tired drains in storefront buildings

Downtown buildings with shops below and apartments above share drain stacks that have served many tenants. Grease, paper and age combine to cause backups.

Plumbing in Mechanicville's Older Homes

Most streets in Mechanicville are lined with wood frame two family houses, narrow singles and a few brick blocks downtown. The original plumbing was simple, and decades of updates added copper, plastic and newer fixtures on top of old cast iron and galvanized lines. Low cellars and tight crawl spaces mean we work carefully and in close quarters.

Homes near the Hudson and the Anthony Kill sit on lower ground, so cellar dampness and groundwater are common concerns. We pay attention to how drains, floor drains and water heaters are set up in those basements.
