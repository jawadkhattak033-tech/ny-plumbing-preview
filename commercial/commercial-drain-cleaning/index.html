Commercial drains carry far more than a household line. A busy restroom bank, a production sink or a row of floor drains can see hundreds of uses a day. Small amounts of paper, grease, soap and grit add up quickly, and when the line finally closes, every fixture upstream backs up at once.

We bring equipment built for that load. Larger drum machines handle three and four inch lines, sectional cables reach long horizontal runs, and a jetter scours grease and scale off the pipe walls. When a clog keeps returning, we can run a camera to show you whether the real issue is buildup, a belly in the pipe or damage.

Our goal is to get you open and keep you open. We explain what we pulled from the line and how your staff can help prevent the next clog, whether that is better trash cans in restrooms, strainers on floor drains or a regular cleaning schedule. Small changes like these often add months between calls.

Warning signs

Signs Your Business Needs Drain Cleaning

  • Floor drains in kitchens, basements or mechanical rooms hold standing water.
  • Several restroom fixtures back up or gurgle at the same time.
  • Sewer odors drift into hallways, dining rooms or sales floors.
  • Water comes up through the lowest drain in the building.
  • Staff have to plunge the same toilet or sink every week.
  • Drains slow down noticeably during peak hours.

Local know how

Why Commercial Drains Clog in New York Buildings

Older commercial blocks across New York often drain through original cast iron that has scaled heavily on the inside. Many of these buildings now hold more restrooms, kitchens and tenants than they were designed for, so the main drain carries much more than it was sized to handle decades ago.

Winter adds sand and road salt tracked in on boots, which washes into floor drains and settles in low spots of the line. Spring thaw and heavy rain can also push groundwater into older drains. A cleaning after the winter season helps many businesses avoid a backup when the weather turns.

Pricing

What Drives the Cost of Commercial Drain Cleaning

The biggest factors are line size, length and access. A single restroom line cleared from a nearby cleanout is quicker than a main drain reached from a roof vent or through a long horizontal run. Grease or heavy scale that needs jetting, after hours scheduling and camera work all affect the total.
