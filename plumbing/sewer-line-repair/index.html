Quick answer: Call Brightwater Plumbing and Drain at (518) 555-0142. We camera the sewer line, mark the damaged spot and depth from above ground, and give you a firm price for the repair before digging. Emergency backups are answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and we clear the line first.

The sewer lateral is the pipe that carries everything from your house to the city sewer or septic tank. In most cases the homeowner is responsible for it, often all the way to the street connection. When it breaks, wastewater has nowhere to go, and the first place it shows up is usually the basement floor drain.

Most sewer problems are not the whole pipe. It is one cracked section, one joint that shifted, or one spot where roots got in. A camera shows exactly where that is, and a locator tells us how deep it sits. That lets us dig a small, targeted hole instead of trenching the whole yard.

When a line has damage in many places, a spot repair may not be the best use of your money. In that case we will show you the footage and explain when trenchless replacement or a full new line makes more sense. Paying for three spot repairs in five years rarely beats fixing the whole pipe once.

Warning signs

Signs of a Damaged Sewer Line

  • Sewage backs up into the basement floor drain or lowest toilet.
  • Several drains in the house slow down at the same time.
  • A patch of lawn above the sewer path stays wet or unusually green.
  • You smell sewer gas outside near the foundation or cleanout.
  • Drain cleaning brings back roots, soil or bits of pipe.
  • The line needs clearing every few months to keep working.

How it works

How We Repair a Sewer Line

Clear and Camera

If the line is backed up, we open it first so you have working drains, then run a camera to see the condition of the full pipe.

Locate and Mark

A locator picks up the camera head from above ground, so we can mark the exact spot and depth of the damage on your property.

Repair the Section

We excavate only what is needed, cut out the failed pipe and install new pipe with proper couplings, bedding and slope.

Test and Restore

A final camera pass confirms the repair. We backfill, tamp the soil and leave the area as tidy as we found it.

Local know how

Sewer Line Trouble in New York Neighborhoods

Older neighborhoods across the state often still have sewer laterals made of clay tile or a tar paper pipe called Orangeburg, laid decades ago. Clay joints let roots in, and Orangeburg slowly flattens and blisters under the weight of soil. Both fail with age, and both are common in homes built before the 1970s.

Freeze and thaw cycles play a part too. Ground that heaves in winter and settles in spring can shift a joint or crack a brittle pipe. Many municipalities also require a permit and inspection for work on the lateral, and we handle that paperwork as part of the job.

Pricing

What Drives the Cost of Sewer Line Repair

The main factors are how deep the pipe is, what sits above it and how long the damaged section runs. A shallow break under lawn is a simpler dig than one under a driveway, sidewalk or mature tree. Pipe material, permit requirements and frozen ground in winter can also affect the work.
