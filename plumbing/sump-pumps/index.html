Quick answer: Call Brightwater Plumbing and Drain at (518) 555-0142 if your sump pump has failed or water is coming into the basement. We answer 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, diagnose the pump, float, check valve and discharge, and repair or replace it with a firm price before any work starts.

A sump pump is easy to forget until the day it matters most. It sits in a pit at the lowest point of the basement and pumps out groundwater before it can rise over the floor. When the float sticks, the motor burns out or the discharge line freezes, water has nowhere to go but up.

We check the whole system, not just the pump. That means the float switch, the check valve that stops water from running back into the pit, the discharge pipe and where it drains outside, and the electrical outlet it runs on. Many failures come from one of those parts rather than the motor.

If the pump needs replacing, we size it to how much water your pit sees and set it up so it is easy to service. We can also add a battery backup pump for storms that knock out the power, and a high water alarm that warns you before the pit overflows.

Warning signs

Signs Your Sump Pump Needs Service

  • The pump runs constantly, even in dry weather.
  • It hums but does not move water out of the pit.
  • Water stays high in the pit or spills onto the floor.
  • The pump cycles on and off every few seconds.
  • You hear grinding, rattling or loud clunks when it starts.
  • The pump is many years old and has never been tested.

How it works

How We Service a Sump Pump

Check the Pit

We look at water level, debris and how fast the pit fills, which tells us how hard the pump has to work in your basement.

Test Each Part

The float, motor, check valve, power supply and discharge line are tested one by one to find the part that is failing.

Repair or Replace

We fix the failed part or install a new pump sized for your water flow, with a fresh check valve and secure discharge connections.

Run It Under Load

We fill the pit with water, watch the pump cycle and confirm water leaves the house and drains away from the foundation.

Local know how

Why Sump Pumps Work Hard in New York

Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can push groundwater up fast, especially in homes built near rivers, streams or in low areas with a high water table. Many basements across the state rely on a sump pump for weeks each spring, and a pump that sat idle all winter can fail just when the melt begins.

Winter brings its own risk. Discharge lines that run above ground can freeze solid, which blocks the pump even when it is running. Summer thunderstorms often knock out power right when the pit is filling. A protected discharge line and a battery backup address both of these common local problems.

Pricing

What Drives the Cost of Sump Pump Work

A float or check valve replacement is a shorter visit than a full pump replacement. The pump type and capacity, the depth and condition of the pit, the length and route of the discharge line and whether you want a battery backup all affect the price. An emergency visit during a storm may also factor in.
