Quick answer: Call Brightwater Plumbing and Drain at (518) 555-0142 to talk through a tankless water heater. We check your gas supply, venting and hot water demand, recommend a unit size, and give you a firm installed price. We also service and descale existing tankless units.

A tankless water heater heats water only when you open a hot tap. Cold water flows through a heat exchanger, a burner or element fires, and hot water comes out the other side. There is no tank to keep warm all day, and no limit on how long the hot water lasts, as long as demand stays within the unit's capacity.

The trade off is in the install. Gas tankless units need a larger gas supply than most tank heaters, and they vent through special pipe, often out a side wall. Electric models can need a significant electrical service upgrade. We check all of this before recommending one, so there are no surprises.

Tankless heaters also need regular care. Mineral scale builds up in the heat exchanger, especially with hard water. A periodic descaling flush keeps the unit efficient and avoids error codes and shutdowns. We install service valves on every unit so that flush takes minutes, and we can set you up with a simple reminder schedule based on how hard your water is.

Warning signs

Signs a Tankless Water Heater May Fit Your Home

  • Your household regularly runs out of hot water at busy times.
  • Your tank heater is near the end of its life and you want an upgrade.
  • You want to free up floor space in a basement or utility room.
  • You are adding a bathroom or remodeling and need more hot water.
  • You plan to stay in your home long enough to value a long lasting unit.
  • Your existing tankless unit shows error codes or lukewarm water.

Local know how

Sizing Tankless Heaters for New York Winters

A tankless heater's output depends on how much it has to raise the water temperature. In much of New York, incoming water gets very cold in January and February, sometimes near freezing on well systems. A unit that easily handles two showers in summer may struggle in winter if it was sized too small. We size for the coldest months.

Hard water is common in many parts of the state, particularly on private wells. Scale coats the heat exchanger faster in hard water, so we often suggest pairing a tankless heater with a water softener or a scale filter, and setting a regular descaling schedule.

What Drives the Cost of a Tankless Water Heater

The unit's capacity and whether it is gas or electric set the starting point. Installation work often matters more. Upsizing a gas line, running new venting through a wall, adding a condensate drain or upgrading electrical service all add to the job. Converting from a tank also means new piping and mounting.

We look at every one of those pieces before giving you a firm installed price, so the quote is complete.
